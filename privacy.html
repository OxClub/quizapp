🧠 OxQuiz — Privacy Policy

Short version: OxQuiz does not collect your personal data. We don't ask for your name, location, contacts, or any account. Quiz scores are stored only on your own device.

1. Who we are

OxQuiz is a free quiz application developed by OxClub ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

2. Information the app handles

  • Scores, XP and settings — stored locally on your device only. We cannot see them and they never leave your phone.
  • Internet connection — used only to download quiz questions. No personal data is transmitted by us.
  • No account, no registration — the app never asks you to sign up.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve ads, according to Google's own policies. We recommend reviewing Google's policy:

https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

You may reset advertising identifiers in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Reset / Opt out).

4. Children

OxQuiz is a general-audience quiz app and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

5. What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell or share your data.
  • We do not access your photos, contacts, files or location.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

6. Permissions

The app requests only the Internet permission, required to load quiz questions and ads.

7. Changes to this policy

If we update the app in ways that affect privacy, we will update this page.
